Tentang

I focus on cyber security, with an emphasis on OSINT, SOCMINT, HUMINT, Threat Intelligence, and OPSEC. My interest lies in analyzing and correlating information from multiple sources to better understand cyber threats and emerging risks. On a practical level, I work with open source data related to phishing activity, suspicious or lookalike domains, and digital footprints that may be misused. I maintain strong awareness of OPSEC and responsible information handling. While I have some interest in penetration testing, it is not my main focus and is pursued primarily for learning and personal development. My current goal is to continue strengthening analytical skills, developing an all source intelligence mindset, and maintaining an ethical and legal approach in all cyber security related activities.
