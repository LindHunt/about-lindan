01 / profile

Security work with a point of view.

ABOUT.TXT

I look at systems the way an attacker might—then make the findings useful for the people defending them.

I’m Lindan Tri Saputra, a cybersecurity practitioner focused on offensive security. My work covers web application and network penetration testing, vulnerability research, OSINT, SOCMINT, and threat intelligence.

I identify and validate vulnerabilities in a realistic way and report them through responsible disclosure. My reports have been recognized by international organizations and institutions, and I’m credited with CVE-2026-18741 and CVE-2026-26211.
