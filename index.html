I map attack surfaces, validate vulnerabilities, and report them the right way.

I'm Lindan Tri Saputra, a cyber security practitioner focused on offensive security. My work covers web application and network penetration testing, vulnerability research, and OSINT and SOCMINT investigations, supported by threat intelligence.

I identify and validate vulnerabilities in a realistic way and report them through responsible disclosure. Reports of mine have been recognized by international organizations and institutions, and I'm credited with CVE-2026-18741 and CVE-2026-26211.

  1. 01

    Analyze the attack surface

    Map what is exposed before anything gets tested.

  2. 02

    Identify and validate

    Confirm that a finding is real, and confirm it in a realistic way.

  3. 03

    Assess the risk

    Rate severity with CVSS v4.0 and describe how to mitigate it.

  4. 04

    Disclose responsibly

    Report to the owner through their disclosure program or CSIRT.
